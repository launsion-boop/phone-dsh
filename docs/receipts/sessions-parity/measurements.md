## How to read the composites

`side-by-side-<state>.png`: iOS baseline on the left, the Android capture on the right,
**Android top-padded by 177 px (59 pt)** so both frames share the content origin. The iOS
status bar and the capture harness's blue-grey ✕ stay visible on the left; the Android
column's top 59 pt is padding, not page UI. Horizontal scale is identical (1 pt = 3 px on
both), so any x difference is a real layout difference — including the *deliberate* one:
the iOS shots are the 320 pt drawer body (separators and CTA end at x 304) while Android
routes the page full width (they end at x 386).

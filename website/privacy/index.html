Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-11-v1

Operator: 龙树(深圳)人工智能有限公司

This policy explains how 龙树(深圳)人工智能有限公司 (“we”) processes personal information when providing the Tono AI website, device connections, and related services, and the choices available to you. You can read this policy without signing in.

1. Account and essential service information

To authenticate and maintain your account, we process your phone number, verification information, account identifier, display name, sessions, and device and network security information. Codes verify sign-in. Your browser stores secure sessions and some interface information, as explained in the Cookie Policy. We also retain the policy version, document digest and server-recorded time of your explicit acceptance by account to document your choice and request renewed acceptance after policy updates.

2. Conversations, audio, and generated content

Depending on the features you enable, we process messages, files and instructions you submit; device audio, transcripts, capture times, sound direction and speaker evidence; and replies, Recap content, memories, task activity and results. Content may include sensitive information about you or others. Speaker recognition is not account authentication. Recording, voiceprint and other-person features require you to understand their purposes and permissions and obtain necessary notices and consent. The sign-in checkbox does not replace these permissions.

3. External accounts, computers, and scope

When you connect an app, mailbox, document source or computer, the relevant services process data, connection identifiers, credentials or authorization status within the connection permissions. A provider may grant account-wide access: selecting a file in our product does not mean the provider permission is limited to that file. Review the authorization screen and local computer scope, and connect only data you are entitled to use. Disconnecting differs from deleting previously obtained conversations, tasks or memories, which may remain.

3.1 Gmail data in Phone DSH

You can choose to connect a Google Account in Phone DSH for iPhone and grant read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) on Google’s consent screen. Phone DSH reads your email address to identify the connected account. It reads message identifiers, senders, subjects and dates to display your inbox and search results. When you open a message, the app retrieves it and displays its body when a preview is available. These features let you browse, search and read your email.

Your iPhone connects directly to the Gmail API over HTTPS. Search terms go directly to Google to run the search. Phone DSH holds message lists and bodies in app memory for the current Gmail browsing session and does not cache them on disk. Google Sign-In manages authorization credentials in the iOS Keychain so the app can restore the connection. The Gmail feature does not transmit Gmail message content or Google authorization tokens to our servers, model providers or MCP services. We do not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising or AI model training.

Choose Disconnect on the Gmail screen to clear the local Gmail sign-in state and current search results. Phone DSH also asks Google to revoke access. If Google cannot complete revocation, you can remove Phone DSH from your Google Account connections. For questions or deletion requests about Gmail data, contact services@longtreeai.com.

Google Account connections

Phone DSH complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, when using or transferring information it receives through Google APIs.

4. Purposes and model training

We process relevant information to authenticate you, understand requests, perform authorized work, generate replies and Recap content, maintain the memories you choose, protect security, troubleshoot and handle rights requests. Task processing, long-term memory, product analytics and model training are different purposes. Sign-in acceptance is not separate consent for model training or nonessential analytics. This policy does not make an unconditional no-training promise covering all third-party services. Submitting content to model providers involves their data-handling rules; excluding training does not necessarily exclude processing or retention. Any request for an optional purpose must describe its purpose and choice rather than rely on this sign-in checkbox.

5. Recipients, cloud processing, and transfers

Tono AI uses cloud services. Depending on the feature, relevant information may be processed by model, speech, cloud computing, database, storage and connected-tool providers. Current related services include DeepSeek, Volcengine and Alibaba Cloud; each request does not go to every provider. Email, file sharing, external tools and computer actions may also disclose content to designated recipients. Review destinations and scope in permissions and confirmations; this policy does not mean every model call displays a prompt. Third-party terms, processing locations and retention rules vary. Processing that requires additional notice or separate consent must follow applicable requirements. Copies already received by others may be impossible to recall.

6. Memory and your controls

Creating memories and using them in conversations are managed separately, subject to the controls currently available. Turning a control off does not automatically delete existing conversations or memories. Disabling automatic creation does not exclude explicit requests to remember something or manual additions. Use available review, correction, deletion and forgetting controls, or email us for requests you cannot complete in the interface. Forgetting does not recall replies already displayed, spoken or delivered; information already in the same conversation may still be used.

7. Retention, deletion, and backups

Retention varies with the data, service need, authorization, applicable legal duties and security purposes. Deleting a memory, deleting a source, disconnecting and closing an account affect different data. Cleanup may involve online records, derivatives, indexes, logs, backups and third-party copies; immediate removal of every copy is not guaranteed. Estimated dates in receipts do not prove every copy has been cleared. Consult the relevant receipt or contact us about outstanding or uncovered copies. This policy does not promise a single unverified retention period. Policy acceptance records may be retained separately where necessary to demonstrate consent and meet applicable duties; this does not mean retaining the associated conversation or audio content.

8. Staff access and security

Support, troubleshooting, security incidents and lawful requests may require authorized personnel to access relevant information. We do not claim that staff can never access data or that tool-call logs constitute complete staff-access auditing. We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the risks. Transport or storage encryption does not mean end-to-end encryption, and no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Requests, withdrawal, and complaints

You may request information about processing, access or a copy, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent and account closure. Use available controls or email services@longtreeai.com with the request type and relevant account. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unrelated sensitive content. We may verify necessary identity and scope and will handle the request or explain limitations under applicable requirements. Withdrawal can affect features relying on that data and does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to a competent authority.

10. Minors and other people

Minors should use the service with guardian guidance and any required consent. Ordinary sign-in acceptance does not authorize processing personal information of children under fourteen or sensitive information such as voiceprints. Do not record, upload or analyze another person’s information without necessary rights or consent. Contact us about information involving minors or unauthorized processing.

11. Policy changes

This page identifies the version and effective date. Material changes to purposes, data categories, recipients or rights will be communicated appropriately, with renewed consent where required. If you disagree, stop the relevant feature and submit any data-handling requests.

Contact and rights requests

services@longtreeai.com

隐私政策

生效日期 2026-11-11 · 版本 2026-11-11-v1

运营主体：龙树(深圳)人工智能有限公司

本政策说明龙树(深圳)人工智能有限公司（“我们”）在提供 Tono AI 网页、设备接入和相关服务时，如何处理个人信息，以及你可以作出的选择。只阅读本政策不需要登录。

1. 账号与必要服务信息

登录和维护账号需要处理手机号、验证码校验信息、账号标识、显示名、登录会话、设备及网络安全信息。验证码用于验证登录；浏览器保存安全会话及部分界面资料，详见 Cookie 政策。我们还会按账号保存你主动同意的政策版本、正文摘要和服务器记录的同意时间，以证明告知与选择，并在政策更新后要求重新确认。

2. 对话、声音与生成内容

根据你启用的功能，我们会处理你提交的消息、文件和指示，设备采集的音频、转写、采集时间、声音方向与人物证据，以及回复、AI日记、长期记忆、任务过程和结果。内容可能涉及你或他人，可能包含敏感信息。声音人物判断不等于账号身份。使用录音、声纹或涉及他人的功能时，应先了解具体用途和权限，并取得必要的告知与同意；登录勾选不能代替这些授权。

3. 外部账号、电脑与授权范围

你连接应用、邮箱、文档或电脑后，相关服务会按连接权限处理资料、连接标识、访问凭据或授权状态。提供方支持的授权粒度可能是账号级；在产品中选择一个文件，不代表提供方授予的权限只覆盖该文件。请核对授权页面与本机范围，仅连接你有权使用的资料。断开连接与删除此前取得的对话、任务或记忆不同，已取得的内容可能仍然保留。

3.1 Phone DSH 的 Gmail 数据

你可以在 iPhone 上的 Phone DSH 中选择连接 Google 账号，并在 Google 的授权页面授予 Gmail 只读权限（gmail.readonly）。Phone DSH 读取账号邮箱地址，用于显示当前连接的账号。显示收件箱和搜索结果时，App 读取邮件标识、发件人、主题和日期。你打开某封邮件时，App 会获取该邮件并显示可预览的正文。此功能用于浏览、搜索和阅读邮件。

iPhone 通过 HTTPS 直接向 Google Gmail API 发送请求。你输入的搜索条件直接交给 Google 执行。Phone DSH 在当前 Gmail 浏览会话的运行内存中保留邮件列表和正文，不为这些内容建立磁盘缓存。Google Sign-In 在 iOS 钥匙串中管理授权凭据，以便恢复连接。Phone DSH 的 Gmail 功能不向我们的服务器、模型服务或 MCP 服务传送 Gmail 邮件内容或 Google 授权令牌。我们不出售 Gmail 数据，也不将其用于广告或 AI 模型训练。

在 Phone DSH 的 Gmail 页面选择「断开连接」，App 会清除本机 Gmail 登录状态和当前搜索结果，并请求 Google 撤销授权。如果 Google 未能完成撤销，你也可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接页面移除 Phone DSH。有关 Gmail 数据的查询或删除请求，可发至 services@longtreeai.com。

4. 处理用途与模型训练

我们处理相关信息以验证身份、理解请求、执行获授权的工作、生成回复和AI日记、维护你选择的记忆，以及保障安全、排障和响应权利请求。完成任务、形成长期记忆、产品分析和模型训练是不同用途。本次登录同意不构成将内容用于模型训练或非必要分析的单独授权。本政策不作覆盖所有第三方服务的无条件“不训练”承诺；向模型服务提交内容涉及其数据处理规则，不训练也不等于不处理或不留存。若另行请求可选用途，将说明用途与选择，不以本次勾选替代。

5. 接收方、云端处理与对外发送

Tono AI 使用云端服务。依所用功能，必要内容可能交由模型、语音、云计算、数据库、存储及你连接的工具服务处理；当前相关服务包括 DeepSeek、火山引擎及阿里云。并非每次请求都发送给全部服务。发送邮件、分享文件、调用外部工具或电脑操作还可能让指定接收方取得内容。请在授权与确认中核对目的地和范围；本政策不表示每次模型调用都会弹窗。第三方适用其自身条款，处理地点和留存规则可能不同；涉及依法需要另行告知或单独同意的处理，应按相应要求办理。已由接收方取得的副本可能无法收回。

6. 记忆和用户控制

生成记忆与在对话中使用记忆分别管理；以当前功能提供的控制为准。关闭开关不自动删除已有会话或记忆，关闭自动生成也不排除你明确要求“记一下”或手动添加。你可使用已开放的查看、更正、删除和遗忘入口；无法在页面处理的请求可发至联系邮箱。遗忘不收回此前已显示、播报或交付的答案，同一段对话已有上下文仍可能被沿用。

7. 保存、删除与备份

不同数据因服务需要、授权、适用法定义务及安全目的有不同保存安排。删除记忆、删除来源、断开连接和注销账号的范围并不相同。清理可能涉及在线记录、派生内容、索引、日志、备份和第三方副本，不能保证立即清除全部副本。页面回执中的预计期限不是所有副本已经清除的证明；尚未覆盖或仍在处理的部分，应结合相应回执或向我们查询。我们不在本政策中承诺尚未核实的统一保留天数。为证明同意和履行适用义务，政策同意记录可在必要范围内单独保留；这不意味着保留对应的对话或录音正文。

8. 人工访问与安全

支持、排障、安全事件或依法处理请求可能需要授权人员接触相关信息。我们不会以“员工绝不可能看到”描述现有服务，也不将普通工具调用日志等同于完整的员工访问审计。我们采取与风险相适应的技术和管理措施；网络或存储加密不等于端到端加密，任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。

9. 你的请求、撤回与投诉

你可以请求了解个人信息处理情况、访问或取得副本、更正、删除、撤回同意及注销账号。优先使用相应功能的已开放入口，也可发送邮件至 services@longtreeai.com，说明请求类型与相关账号；请勿发送密码、验证码或无关敏感正文。我们可能核实必要身份和请求范围，并按适用要求处理或解释限制。撤回可能影响依赖相应数据的功能，不影响此前合法处理；你仍可向有权机关投诉。

10. 未成年人及其他人的信息

未成年人应在监护人指导和必要同意下使用服务。不满十四周岁儿童的个人信息、声纹等敏感个人信息不因普通登录勾选而获得处理授权。请勿在缺少必要权利或同意时记录、上传或分析他人的信息；发现涉及未成年人或未授权信息的问题，请联系我们。

11. 政策变更

我们会在本页标明版本和生效日期。处理目的、信息种类、接收方或用户权利等发生重大变化时，将通过适当方式告知，并在依法需要时重新征求同意。不同意的用户可停止相关功能并提出数据处理请求。

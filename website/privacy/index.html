Phone DSH Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

龙树(深圳)人工智能有限公司 operates the Phone DSH app and this website. This policy explains how Phone DSH handles data when you use these services.

1. Google Account and Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. When you choose to connect it on iPhone, Google Sign-In requests basic Google Account identity information and the Gmail read-only permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). Phone DSH shows the connected Gmail address so you can identify the mailbox. It uses message identifiers to retrieve the senders and subjects shown in the inbox and search results. It shows the date when you open a message. Google runs the search using the terms you enter. The app retrieves and displays a message body when you open that message. Email previews omit attachments and remote content.

Where Gmail replies are available, Google Sign-In requests the send permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) when you first confirm a reply. The iPhone uses the original message’s reply address, subject and thread identifiers to send your confirmed reply through Gmail. Google delivers the reply text to the recipient you reviewed and keeps the sent message in your Gmail account. Phone DSH holds unsent reply text in memory until you close the reply or send it. The Gmail connector does not archive, delete, or change existing messages.

The iPhone sends requests directly to Google over HTTPS. Phone DSH keeps Gmail search results and message bodies in app memory while you use the connector and does not cache them on disk. Google Sign-In stores authorization credentials in the iOS Keychain so the app can restore the connection. Phone DSH uses Google user data solely to connect the mailbox and provide the Gmail features described here.

The Gmail connector does not send Google user data, including account information, search terms, message headers, message bodies, authorization credentials, or data derived from them, to our servers, Phone DSH agents, AI model providers, model gateways, or MCP services. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising or to create, train, or improve AI or machine learning models. These limits apply to the Gmail connector regardless of which model provider you configure for other Phone DSH features.

Select Disconnect in Gmail to clear the local connection and search results. Phone DSH also requests revocation from Google. You can remove access directly in your Google Account connections, including if revocation could not be completed on the phone.

Phone DSH uses and transfers information received from Google APIs in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy.

2. Other Phone DSH features

When you use AI features, Phone DSH can send the prompts, files and other context you choose to provide to the model service you configure. When you use a device connection or a third-party tool, it receives the information needed for the operation you request. These services have their own privacy policies and retention rules. This section concerns data you provide to those features. The Gmail connector's data handling is described in section 1.

Conversation history, provider settings and device connection settings can be stored on your device so you can use them again. You can remove items through the applicable app controls. For a copy, correction or deletion you cannot complete in the app, contact us. Information already sent to a service you chose is subject to that service's controls.

3. Website and support

This website stores your language and theme choices in your browser. Web infrastructure processes standard request information, such as IP addresses and requested paths, to deliver and protect the site. If you contact us, we use your contact details and the information you provide to respond to your request.

4. Your choices and contact

To ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent, email services@longtreeai.com. Include the affected feature and the type of request. Keep passwords and verification codes private. We will post policy updates here and notify you of material changes where required.

Phone DSH 隐私政策

生效日期 2026-11-13

龙树(深圳)人工智能有限公司运营 Phone DSH 应用及本网站。本政策说明你使用这些服务时，Phone DSH 如何处理相关数据。

1. Google 账号和 Gmail

连接 Gmail 由你选择。你在 iPhone 上发起连接时，Google 登录会请求基本账号身份信息及 Gmail 只读权限（https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly）。Phone DSH 显示当前连接的 Gmail 地址，方便你确认邮箱。App 用邮件标识获取收件箱和搜索结果中显示的发件人和主题，打开邮件时显示日期。Google 根据你输入的条件执行搜索。你打开某封邮件时，App 才获取并显示该邮件正文。邮件预览不下载附件或加载远程内容。

在支持 Gmail 回复的版本中，首次确认发送回复时，Google 登录会请求发送权限（https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send）。iPhone 使用原邮件的回复地址、主题和会话标识，通过 Gmail 发送你确认的回复。Google 将回复正文送达你核对的收件人，并在你的 Gmail 账号中保留已发送邮件。Phone DSH 将未发送的回复保留在内存中，关闭回复页面或发送后即清除。Gmail 连接器不会归档、删除或修改已有邮件。

iPhone 通过 HTTPS 直接向 Google 发出请求。Phone DSH 在你使用 Gmail 连接器时将搜索结果和邮件正文保留在 App 运行内存中，不在磁盘上缓存。Google 登录将授权凭据保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，用于恢复连接。Phone DSH 仅使用 Google 用户数据连接邮箱和提供本节所述的 Gmail 功能。

Gmail 连接器不向我们的服务器、Phone DSH Agent、AI 模型服务商、模型网关或 MCP 服务传送 Google 用户数据，包括账号信息、搜索条件、邮件标头、邮件正文、授权凭据及其衍生数据。我们不出售这些数据，不将其用于广告，也不用于创建、训练或改进 AI 或机器学习模型。你为 Phone DSH 其他功能配置的模型服务不影响这些限制。

在 Gmail 页面选择断开连接会清除本机连接和搜索结果。Phone DSH 同时向 Google 请求撤销访问。如果手机上的撤销请求未完成，你也可以直接在 Google 账号连接中移除访问权限。

Phone DSH 对从 Google API 获取的信息的使用和传输遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策及其中的有限使用要求，也遵守 Google Workspace API 用户数据和开发者政策。

2. Phone DSH 的其他功能

使用 AI 功能时，Phone DSH 可能把你选择提供的提示词、文件和其他上下文发送给你配置的模型服务。使用设备连接或第三方工具时，相应服务会收到执行你所请求操作所需的信息。这些服务有各自的隐私政策和留存规则。本节适用于你向这些功能提供的数据。Gmail 连接器的数据处理见第 1 节。

对话记录、模型服务设置和设备连接设置可以保存在你的设备上，以便下次使用。你可以通过 App 中相应的控件移除相关内容。若无法在 App 中完成查阅、复制、更正或删除，请联系我们。已发送给你所选服务的信息，适用该服务的管理方式。

3. 网站与支持

本网站在你的浏览器中保存语言和主题选择。网站基础设施会处理 IP 地址和访问路径等标准请求信息，用于提供和保护网站。你联系我们时，我们使用你提供的联系方式和信息来处理请求。

4. 你的选择与联系方式

如果你想了解数据处理情况、申请查阅、更正、删除，或撤回同意，请发邮件至 services@longtreeai.com，并说明相关功能和请求类型。请妥善保管密码和验证码。政策更新会在此页公布，重大变更将按要求通知。
